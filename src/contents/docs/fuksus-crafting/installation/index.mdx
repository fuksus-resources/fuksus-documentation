## Before installing

<Note type="warning" title="Warning">
  Make sure your server's artifacts are updated to 4752 or higher!
</Note>

## Installation steps

<Stepper>
  <StepperItem title="Step 1: Download resource from keymaster">
    Go to your [keymaster](https://keymaster.fivem.net/) page and download `fuksus-crafting`.
  </StepperItem>
  <StepperItem title="Step 2: Place resource in your resources folder">
    Grab your `fuksus-crafing.pack.zip` and excract it in your `server/resources/` folder.
  </StepperItem>
  <StepperItem title="Step 3: Configure your framework">
    Check out the bridge folders and configure your framework.
    > These frameworks are preconfigured:
    - ESX
    - QBCore
    - QBox
  </StepperItem>
  <StepperItem title="Step 4: Enjoy your new resource">
    Enjoy your new high quality resource!
  </StepperItem>
</Stepper>
